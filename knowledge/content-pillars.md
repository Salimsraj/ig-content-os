# Content pillars

Sourced 2026-08-16 from a real produced reel script shared by Salim — not invented. Confirmed pillar so

## GTM Engineering education

Explaining GTM Engineering concepts to a technical Arabic-speaking audience who haven't encountered the
term. Confirmed structural pattern from the real example:

1. Open with a direct audience call-out ("if you're a designer, developer, or startup/scaleup founder...").
2. Define the concept plainly (commercial strategy + technical execution → design/build/integrate/scale
   systems for reach/market/delivery).
3. Make it concrete with a real system example (e.g. a LinkedIn/Instagram prospecting system that finds
   the right person, drafts a personal email, sends it automatically, and schedules the best time/way to
   follow up).
4. Ground it with a real regional reference (e.g. a named Arab business figure discussing the topic on a
   named podcast) — real citations, not invented ones.
5. Frame the market gap/opportunity (role pays $50k+/year minimum, very few qualified people, no
   university teaches it, few regional experts).
6. Close with a resource/lead-magnet CTA: "comment [keyword] and I'll send you the resources" (see
   `cta-rules.md`).
