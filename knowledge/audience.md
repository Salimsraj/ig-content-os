# Audience

Sourced 2026-08-16 from a real produced reel script (GTM Engineering explainer) shared by Salim — not
invented.

- **Who:** Designers (مصمم), developers/programmers (مبرمج), and startup/scaleup founders (فاوندر
  لستارت أب أو سكيل أب) — specifically Arabic-speaking (Shami dialect).
- **What they don't know yet, that the content teaches:** what GTM Engineering actually is — combining
  commercial strategy with technical execution to design/build/integrate/scale systems that help a
  company reach, market to, and deliver product to any market.
- **Why this gap exists for them:** no university teaches it, most practitioners learn it purely from
  experience, and there are very few GTM experts in the Arab world specifically — the content exists to
  close that gap.
